Cybersecurity Awareness and Protection Guide
Introduction;cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, network, online accounts and personal information from digital threats. This project was created to help beginners understand common cybersecurity risks and learn simple ways to stay safe online.
Project objectives;explain common cybersecurity threats in simple language,help users identify suspicious emails and messages.
Common cybersecurity threats;phishing which is a cyberattacks in which criminals use fake emails to trick people into giving them sensitive information,malware which is a harmful software designed to damage devices,steal information and gain unauthorized access.
Cybersecurity protection measures;creating strong and unique passwords,avoiding suspicious inks and regular back ups
